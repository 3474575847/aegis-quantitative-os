# Research Experimentation Engine

The Experimentation Engine in Aegis-Alpha provides a deterministic and structured layer for managing, comparing, and querying quantitative research workflows.

## Core Components (Phase 1)

### 1. Experiment Registry Foundation

Manages the lifecycle and versioning of research experiments. It ensures that every experiment run is uniquely identified and linked to its constituent workflows.

### 2. Persistence Layer

Immutable storage of experiment runs and metadata in TimescaleDB, ensuring a complete audit trail for all research activities.

## Future Phases

- Phase 2: Factor Comparison Engine, Ranking & Evaluation Layer, Research Query Engine, and Prometheus metrics.
